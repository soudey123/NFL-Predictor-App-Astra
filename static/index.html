NO BLACK BOX

Here’s how
we make the call.

Simple inputs. Visible reasoning.
A starting point for your own football judgment.

01 Start with team strength

We use 2025 regular-season points scored, points allowed, and record. Home margin = 0.65 × the difference in per-game point differential + 6 × the difference in win rate + 1.5 points for home field (zero at neutral venues).

02 Turn the margin into a forecast

Home win probability = 1 ÷ (1 + exp(−margin ÷ 7)), capped at 5–95%. Expected total points average both offenses against the opposing defenses, bounded at 28–65. We split that total by the margin and round; a rounded tie adds one point to the predicted winner.

03 Change the story

Scenario controls subtract 0–7 points for a missing QB and add 0.25 points per extra rest day (0–4). These are your assumptions, not a live injury feed. Scenarios never overwrite the baseline used by the scoreboard.

Model v1.0 · Heuristic, not calibrated. No current-season form, roster changes, weather, or automatic injury adjustments. Actual results update when you refresh each week. Accuracy is measured on stored pregame forecasts; late-loaded games are excluded.
